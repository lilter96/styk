# MoySklad populated test-account validation

Status: **populated fixture validation completed for the scenarios below**, including variants. The owner authorized synthetic writes to the previously confirmed isolated test account, confirmed that the current configuration points to that account, and confirmed that its integrations, automations and notifications cannot propagate the proposed fixtures to production or real recipients. This is an operator attestation; the API cannot prove the absence of every external integration. These observations do not establish production readiness, a coherent stock snapshot, or general reservation attribution.

## Preflight evidence

The configured account matched account IDs returned by warehouse and organization reads. Before fixture creation, the API reported one warehouse, one organization, zero products, zero variants, zero customer orders, zero stock entries (`enter`), zero receipts (`supply`), zero shipments (`demand`), and zero inventory documents. The visible webhook and stock-webhook collections both reported zero entries. These counts are permission-scoped API observations, not proof of universal account visibility. No pre-existing entity was renamed, changed or deleted.

The first attempt through the configured proxy timed out. Direct HTTPS succeeded. A direct preflight using an incomplete Accept header returned HTTP 415/400; the existing client convention `Accept: application/json;charset=utf-8` plus gzip succeeded. Credentials and raw responses are not included here.

## API contract and planned fixture

The official specifications describe [product creation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/moysklad/api-remap-1.2-doc/master/md/dictionaries/_product.md), [warehouse creation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/moysklad/api-remap-1.2-doc/master/md/dictionaries/_store.md), [variants](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/moysklad/api-remap-1.2-doc/master/md/dictionaries/_variant.md), [stock entries](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/moysklad/api-remap-1.2-doc/master/md/documents/_enter.md), [customer orders](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/moysklad/api-remap-1.2-doc/master/md/documents/_customerOrder.md), [counterparties](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/moysklad/api-remap-1.2-doc/master/md/dictionaries/_counterparty.md) and [shipments](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/moysklad/api-remap-1.2-doc/master/md/documents/_demand.md). A stock entry requires organization and warehouse references; a customer order requires organization and counterparty references. These are documented contracts, not claims that any write succeeded.

The fixed, namespaced plan contains two warehouses; nine products including a duplicate-article pair, an item without article, a distinctive code and a variant parent; a synthetic counterparty; two stock entries; four orders; two variants; and one partial-shipment scenario. The desired stock entries are warehouse A: product A 10 and product B 5; warehouse B: product A 4 and product C 12. Intended reservations include product A quantity 3/reserve 2 and product B quantity 1/reserve 0. Expected numbers remain hypotheses until independently reported by MoySklad.

The first live variant metadata and characteristic-list responses returned HTTP 200 with `meta` but omitted both `characteristics` and `rows`. The [published documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/moysklad/api-remap-1.2-doc/master/md/dictionaries/_characteristic.md) describes collection, creation and ID-specific reads; the [official Java SDK](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/moysklad/remap-1.2-java-sdk/master/docs/GetVariantCharacteristics200Response.md) models list `rows` as optional. Its absence does not prove an empty or exhaustive collection. Before the continuation, the existing journal showed that the namespaced characteristic and both variants had been skipped before sending. Both documented list forms were checked for visible namespace collisions; their incomplete coverage was not treated as proof of absence. The explicitly authorized continuation made one namespaced creation attempt. The returned characteristic ID was journaled immediately and independently checked by the documented ID-specific GET for its ID, name, type and metadata. If the response had been lost without an ID, the journal would remain unknown and the tool would not resend it.

## Fixture results and observed source behavior

The existing manifest now records 24 `CREATED_AND_VERIFIED` external IDs: two new warehouses, nine products, one fixture-named characteristic, two variants, one synthetic counterparty, three stock entries, five customer orders and one partial-shipment document. The five continuation creations were the characteristic, two variants, a new variant-only stock entry and a new variant order. No prior entity was modified or deleted, and there are no `UNKNOWN_OUTCOME` operations. Both independently retrieved variants belonged to the existing fixture parent, had distinct identities and different fixture-named characteristic values. The duplicate-article products were accepted as distinct products; their separate external IDs and shared article were preserved. The product without an article had that field absent in the source response; the existing product model stores it as an empty string, so absence and an explicit empty string cannot be distinguished. No match is inferred from either.

The stock entries were independently read back. Before shipment, the warehouse report showed physical stock A/A=10, A/B=4, B/A=5 and C/B=12. The linked partial shipment then reported one unit shipped on a two-unit order position. After shipment, bounded polling observed B/A physical stock 4 and reserve 1; A/A remained stock 10 and reserve 2, A/B stock 4 and reserve 0, and C/B stock 12 and reserve 0. The separate variant entry reported variant one/A physical stock 8 and reserve 1, and variant two/A stock 3 and reserve 0, after a new order with variant one quantity 2 and reserve 1. The order's warehouse, position ID, variant assortment ID and source updated timestamp were independently read and compared. Reported incoming values for those pairs were zero. These are observations at different times, not proof that every aggregate reservation is attributable to a particular order or baseline. A separate no-article product had an explicit source-reported zero stock row.

The warehouse-report product links carried an `expand` query parameter. The existing importer extracts identities from the URL path and handled them; the new fixture verifier initially rejected them. It was corrected to validate the official origin and entity path while ignoring the query for identity, with a sanitized regression test. No production importer fix was required.

## Local fixture journal

The in-development fixture tool defaults to a dry plan, uses an exact account confirmation and an isolated-test-account attestation for execution, and keeps a private JSON journal under the ignored `.local/moysklad-fixtures/` directory. It stores a hash of the selected account, synthetic namespace and operation identities, states, initial aggregate counts, and returned remote IDs; it stores neither tokens nor raw responses. Inspect mode suppresses remote IDs in terminal output. The journal uses a per-namespace file lock, private permissions, exclusive initialization, atomic replacement and fsync.

States are `PLANNED`, `FAILED_BEFORE_SEND`, `UNKNOWN_OUTCOME`, `CREATED_AND_VERIFIED` and `SKIPPED`. Before a creation request the journal is durably changed to `UNKNOWN_OUTCOME`. A successful returned ID is saved before independent GET verification. For ordinary fixtures, resume searches the exact namespaced identity; zero or multiple results are uncertain and never trigger a retry. A unique result must also pass independent content verification. Characteristics have a weaker collection contract: with a saved returned ID the tool verifies the ID-specific GET directly; with no saved ID it stays unknown, even if a collection appears empty or contains a candidate. No deletion endpoint is exposed. The client checks account identity, account-scoped counts and API-visible webhooks again before execution. Both explicit confirmation flags and local access control remain operational responsibilities.

From the repository root, `go run ./cmd/moysklad-fixtures` prints a dry plan without network access. `-mode init` creates a private journal for the locally configured `MOYSKLAD_ACCOUNT_ID`. Inspect an existing namespace with `-mode inspect -namespace NAMESPACE`. To execute or resume only after account verification and an explicit isolation attestation, load the token and account ID into the environment, set `STYK_FIXTURE_CONFIRM_ACCOUNT_ID` equal to `MOYSKLAD_ACCOUNT_ID`, set `STYK_FIXTURE_ISOLATED_TEST_ACCOUNT=yes`, then use `-mode execute` or `-mode resume -namespace NAMESPACE`. The explicit `-max-posts`, `-max-requests`, `-interval`, `-request-timeout` and `-timeout` flags bound work. `-mode verify-stock` performs bounded read-only polling and records the observed quantities and time; `STOCK_MATCH` compares physical stock only. Source reservations remain separately reported. A stopped unknown outcome must be investigated through the manifest and independent source reads; do not edit it to `PLANNED` or resend it manually. `enable-shipment`, `enable-multiple`, and `enable-variants` replan only previously skipped, never-sent operations with verified prerequisites; they cannot reset an unknown operation. The variant continuation appends new stock and order operations and freezes the historical product-only order composition. No cleanup is automatic.

## Optional live import comparison

The normal unit and integration test commands never contact MoySklad. To repeat the explicitly authorized read-only comparison, use a migrated disposable **local** PostgreSQL database whose name starts with `styk_fixture_`, register the test account to the fixture tenant there, and set `TEST_DATABASE_URL`, `MOYSKLAD_TOKEN`, `MOYSKLAD_ACCOUNT_ID`, and `STYK_FIXTURE_NAMESPACE` in the environment. Then run `STYK_FIXTURE_LIVE_READ_ONLY=yes go test -count=1 -tags=fixturelive -run '^TestLiveVariantFixtureImport$' -v ./internal/moyskladfixture`. It checks the journal's account hash, makes bounded GET requests with page size one through existing clients, and stores observations only in the designated local database. It must not be enabled without separate authorization for the selected test account. Keep credentials and the private journal out of Git.
